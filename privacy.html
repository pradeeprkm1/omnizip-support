OmniZip Privacy Policy Effective date: November 7, 2026 This policy describes how OmniZip: Zip & Unzip (“OmniZip,” “the app”) handles information. It applies to the iOS app and its related support pages. Files and information you choose to use OmniZip processes files you select to preview, organize, archive, extract, or convert. The app is designed to process these files on your device and does not upload their contents to an OmniZip-operated server. Files may be stored in the app’s Documents folder on your device. If you choose an iOS sharing, export, or cloud-storage destination, iOS and that destination’s provider handle the transfer under their own terms and privacy practices. The app stores its free archive-action count and the date used to reset that count in local app preferences. The app does not require an OmniZip account. Advertising and consent The free version uses Google Mobile Ads. Google’s SDK and its advertising partners may process information such as device or advertising identifiers, IP address and approximate location derived from it, ad views and interactions, app interactions, diagnostics, and performance information to provide, measure, and improve advertising and related services. The information collected and its use can depend on your device settings, region, consent choices, and Google’s configuration. OmniZip uses Google’s User Messaging Platform to present consent and privacy choices where applicable. You can revisit available choices using the app’s Privacy Options control when it is shown. For more information, see Google’s Privacy Policy and Google’s disclosure for the Mobile Ads SDK. Purchases In-app purchases are processed by Apple using StoreKit. OmniZip receives transaction and entitlement information needed to enable or restore Premium features. The app does not receive your Apple Account payment-card details. Apple’s handling of purchase information is governed by Apple’s terms and privacy policy. Information we do not operate services to collect The app does not provide an OmniZip account service or an OmniZip-operated cloud file service. We do not operate a server that receives the files you process in the app. This does not cover information collected by Apple, Google, or a destination you choose to use. Data retention and deletion Files and app preferences remain on your device until you delete them or remove the app. You can delete files from within the app or through iOS Files. Information handled by Apple, Google, or another service is subject to that provider’s retention and deletion practices. Children OmniZip is a general-purpose file utility and is not designed specifically for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information through an OmniZip account service. Changes to this policy We may update this policy when the app’s features, SDKs, or data practices change. The effective date above indicates when this version was last updated.
