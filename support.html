OmniZip Support

We can help with importing files, archive creation and extraction, previews, PDF tools, and Premium purchases.

Before contacting support

  • Include your OmniZip app version and iOS version.
  • Describe the steps that led to the issue and any error message shown.
  • For purchase issues, do not send payment details. Use Apple’s purchase history or subscription settings where appropriate.
